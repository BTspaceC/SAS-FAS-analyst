# Structural market behavior

Use this module to explain durable price behavior and entry risk, not to forecast the next few months.

Compare the asset with the correct benchmark over multi-quarter and multi-year windows. Examine total return, relative strength, drawdown, recovery quality, volatility, correlation changes, and whether price weakness predates fundamental deterioration.

For equities, examine float, average dollar volume, passive index inclusion, short interest, borrow cost, insider sales, buybacks, issuance, convertibles, options reflexivity, and shareholder concentration.

For crypto, examine credible spot liquidity, exchange concentration, staking or locked share, emissions, unlocks, treasury or foundation flows, tagged exchange inflows, derivatives open interest and funding, liquidation cascades, bridge liquidity, and holder concentration.

Separate:

- market beta;
- sector or narrative rotation;
- asset-specific information;
- mechanical supply;
- leverage and liquidity amplification.

Do not infer causality from price correlation alone. Use event timing, flows, disclosed supply, and counterfactual benchmarks. Market structure may change entry pacing or reveal permanent financing risk, but chart patterns alone cannot upgrade the fundamental thesis.
