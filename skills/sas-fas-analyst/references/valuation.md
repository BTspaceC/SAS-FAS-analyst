# Long-horizon valuation

Value three to five years primarily and one to two years secondarily. Use at least two genuinely independent methods when valid, but do not average weak or incompatible methods.

## US equities

Prefer normalized owner earnings or free cash flow, reverse DCF, reinvestment-based DCF, sum-of-parts, asset value, or probability-adjusted pipeline value according to the route. Use comparables as a reasonableness check rather than the sole anchor.

Expose revenue growth, normalized margin, reinvestment, dilution, discount rate, terminal assumptions, and cyclicality. Show what the current price implies. Prevent terminal value from silently carrying the thesis.

## Crypto assets

Do not equate protocol success with token value. Model token-specific capture.

Use route-appropriate combinations of fee or external-revenue multiples, required productive collateral, security budget, monetary premium, resource demand, comparable network value, liquidation or treasury value, and probability-weighted terminal scenarios.

Explicitly model dilution, holder capture, governance leakage, substitute tokens, and the probability that usage grows without benefiting the token.

## Scenario discipline

Build bear, base, and bull scenarios with weights totaling 100%. Include a zero or near-zero outcome when economically plausible. State the evidence that would move weight between scenarios.

Classify the weight basis as `empirical`, `model_based`, `market_implied`, `mixed`, or `judgmental`. Use the word probability only for the first four when the method and supporting evidence are disclosed. Otherwise call the numbers judgmental scenario weights; they structure a decision but do not estimate statistical frequency.

Test at least three plausible weight sets, including a more bearish and a more bullish set. State whether the recommended action is invariant. If the action changes, expose the boundary rather than presenting one preferred set as precise.

Report ranges, not point precision. Calculate probability-weighted value only when scenario inputs are comparable and the weight basis is defensible. Label narrative option value separately.

## Extreme undervaluation

Call an asset extremely undervalued only when evidence grade is A or B, the fundamental base is stable or strengthening, conservative value still shows a large margin of safety, the asset can survive the thesis horizon, the price impairment is plausibly reversible, expected upside is at least three times permanent-loss exposure, and the user's portfolio can withstand a zero.
