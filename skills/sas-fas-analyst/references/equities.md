# US equity analysis

## Route before calculating

Classify the issuer as general operating company, SaaS, bank, insurer, REIT, commodity/cyclical, pre-revenue biotech, or public crypto company. Do not apply metrics outside their valid domain.

## General operating companies

Evaluate revenue quality, normalized margins, cash conversion, reinvestment runway, incremental returns, balance-sheet survival, dilution, capital allocation, customer and supplier concentration, and governance.

- QoE = cash from operations ÷ net income. Flag sign changes and near-zero denominators.
- Sloan total accruals = (net income − cash from operations) ÷ average total assets.
- ROIC = NOPAT ÷ average invested capital. State the NOPAT and invested-capital definitions.
- ROIIC = change in NOPAT ÷ change in invested capital over a period long enough to reduce noise.
- Use Beneish M-Score as a screening signal, never proof of fraud. Explain the contributing variables.
- Use the appropriate Altman variant and skip banks, insurers, and other invalid cases.

## SaaS and subscription businesses

Use growth, gross margin, free-cash-flow margin, NRR, gross retention, CAC payback, LTV/CAC assumptions, SBC dilution, deferred revenue or RPO, and cohort economics.

Treat Rule of 40 as graded:

- 40% or higher: strong combined growth and margin;
- 20% to 40%: mixed and valuation-dependent;
- below 20%: severe warning, not automatic failure.

Do not accept company-defined adjusted profit without reconciling SBC and recurring restructuring.

## Specialized routes

- Banks: use capital adequacy, deposit quality and beta, net interest margin, credit losses, liquidity, duration, and tangible book value. Skip Altman and ordinary industrial ROIC.
- Insurers: use combined ratio, reserve development, float cost, investment risk, solvency capital, and book-value compounding.
- REITs: use AFFO, same-store NOI, occupancy, lease maturities, debt ladder, fixed-rate share, and NAV. Do not anchor on GAAP earnings.
- Commodity/cyclicals: normalize through-cycle price, cost curve, reserve life, sustaining capex, and balance-sheet survival. Do not capitalize peak earnings.
- Pre-revenue biotech: use probability-adjusted pipeline value, trial design, endpoints, safety, cash runway, dilution, patent life, and competitive standard of care.
- Public crypto companies: separate operating-company cash flows from token treasury exposure and token issuance.

## Moat discipline

Test 7 Powers without forcing a category. Distinguish an observed power from a plausible future power. Quantify switching friction, scale curve, network density, retention, price premium, exclusive access, or process advantage. A popular product is not automatically a moat.

Require the bull case to identify reinvestment economics and the bear case to identify the mechanism of permanent impairment, not merely a lower valuation multiple.
